## 1. Executive Summary

**MIRR Construction & Engineering Corporation** (MIRR Build) is an internationally recognized Tier-1 general contractor, civil engineering firm, and pre-construction technology consultancy. Established in 1998, MIRR has built a reputation over nearly three decades for executing monumental, technically complex projects on schedule and within guaranteed maximum price (GMP) contracts.

With a portfolio exceeding **₱180 Billion** in delivered assets across the commercial, industrial, civil infrastructure, and luxury residential sectors, MIRR pairs traditional structural engineering craftsmanship with pioneering 4D/5D Building Information Modeling (BIM), autonomous drone photogrammetry, and green low-carbon concrete technologies.

## 4. Core Divisions & Engineering Capabilities

### A. Commercial High-Rise & Mixed-Use
- Turnkey grade-A corporate towers, retail pavilions, luxury hotels, and biomedical research centers.
- Post-tensioned concrete structural framing, perimeter diagrid engineering, and unitized silicone-glazed curtain wall systems.
- Smart Building Management Systems (BMS), redundant power microgrids, and acoustic vibration dampening.

### B. Industrial & Advanced Logistics Hubs
- High-throughput fulfillment centers, automated guided vehicle (AGV) robotics facilities, and cold storage hubs.
- Super-flat laser-screed concrete flooring (FF/FL 50+ compliance).
- Heavy structural steel clear spans up to 180 feet without interior supporting columns.
- Early Suppression Fast Response (ESFR) automated fire safety and hazardous material containment.

### C. Bespoke Luxury Residential Architecture
- Museum-grade private residential estates, cliffside cantilevers, and modernist waterfront compounds.
- 20-meter cantilevered post-tensioned terraces, frameless motorized glazing, and custom architectural exposed concrete.
- Net-zero residential microgrids, geothermal heat exchange, and smart home automation.

### D. Civil Infrastructure & Municipal Engineering
- Transportation corridors, multimodal bridges, viaducts, airport tarmac facilities, and highway interchanges.
- Deep foundation engineering: cast-in-drilled-hole (CIDH) caissons exceeding 120-foot depths, micropiling, and slurry walls.
- Reinforced retaining wall stabilization (MSE) and high-volume stormwater detention networks.

### E. Pre-Construction, 4D/5D BIM & Drone Telemetry
- Complete federated digital clash detection prior to site mobilization.
- 5D real-time cost loading integrated with automated bills of quantities (BOQ).
- Autonomous aerial drone photogrammetry and LiDAR 3D cloud mapping for millimetric QA/QC audits.

### F. Adaptive Reuse, Historic Preservation & Seismic Retrofit
- Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymer (CFRP) structural column wrapping.
- Tuned mass dampers and viscous hydraulic damper integrations for high-seismic zones.
- Historic masonry repointing, foundation underpinning, and commercial-to-residential conversions.

## 6. Health, Safety, Environment & Quality (HSEQ)

Safety is not a department at MIRR—it is our primary operating condition.

- **OSHA VPP Star Status:** Maintained consecutively since 2014.
- **Experience Modification Rate (EMR):** 0.62 (Industry average is 1.0; significantly lower worker risk and lower insurance overhead passed on to clients).
- **Mandatory 100% Fall Protection:** Strictly enforced tie-off standard above 6 feet.
- **Daily BIM Safety Audits:** Work zones are simulated in 3D prior to high-risk crane picks and trench excavations.
- **Environmental Management:** 85%+ of jobsite debris recycled; low-VOC coatings and low-embodied-carbon cement used across all projects.
