1. Executive Summary

MIRR Construction & Engineering Corporation is a premier Tier-1 general contracting, civil infrastructure, and design-build enterprise. Founded in 1998, MIRR has built an unbroken legacy of engineering landmark commercial high-rises, automated industrial distribution hubs, state-of-the-art transportation infrastructure, and custom luxury residences.

With over ₱180 Billion in capital assets successfully commissioned, MIRR is distinguished by its zero-incident jobsite culture, 99.4% on-time completion milestone rate, and pioneering deployment of 4D/5D Building Information Modeling (BIM) and autonomous drone survey telemetry.
