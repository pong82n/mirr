1. Executive Summary

MIRR Construction Services (MIRR) is an established general building contractor, civil engineering firm, and pre-construction consultancy. Established in 2017, MIRR has built a reputation over nearly a decade of continuous operations for executing technically sound residential and commercial facilities on schedule and within guaranteed maximum price (GMP) contracts.

With a delivered portfolio exceeding ₱180 Million across residential, commercial, and civil infrastructure developments, MIRR pairs traditional structural engineering craftsmanship with modern 4D/5D Building Information Modeling (BIM), autonomous drone photogrammetry, and green low-carbon concrete technologies.
