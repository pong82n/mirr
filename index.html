What We Build

Full-Lifecycle Engineering Services

From pre-construction 4D BIM modeling to structural post-tensioning and turnkey facility handover.

Commercial High-Rises

Grade-A office towers, corporate HQs, and mixed-use retail complexes engineered with structural steel, diagrid perimeter framing, and smart MEP systems.

  • Post-tensioned concrete slabs
  • Smart HVAC & energy BMS
  • Full regulatory & ADA compliance

Industrial Logistics Plants

Turnkey manufacturing plants, automated robotics distribution centers, heavy machinery foundations, and cold-storage distribution hubs.

  • Super-flat laser-screed floors (FF/FL 50+)
  • Clear-span rafters up to 180 ft
  • ESFR automated fire sprinkler grids

Bespoke Luxury Residential

Custom private estates, modernist cantilevered waterfront villas, and sustainable multi-family residences constructed to museum-grade standards.

  • 20-meter cantilevered concrete roofs
  • Motorized floor-to-ceiling glass
  • Net-zero solar & geothermal systems

Civil Infrastructure Works

Roadways, multimodal bridges, deep-drilled caisson foundation works, stormwater detention reservoirs, and municipal earthmoving.

  • Drilled caissons to 120-ft bedrock
  • Prestressed concrete girder bridges
  • GPS-guided heavy earthmoving fleets

Pre-Construction 4D BIM

Federated digital clash detection, algorithmic material scheduling, and drone photogrammetry to guarantee zero-rework execution before ground-break.

  • Zero-clash MEP spatial coordination
  • Automated bill of quantities (BOQ)
  • LiDAR site scanning & 3D cloud twins

Adaptive Seismic Retrofit

Extending structural life cycles. Carbon fiber column wrapping, viscous damper additions, foundation underpinning, and energy conversions.

  • Structural carbon fiber wrapping (CFRP)
  • Deep foundation micropile underpinning
  • Historic façade preservation certification
